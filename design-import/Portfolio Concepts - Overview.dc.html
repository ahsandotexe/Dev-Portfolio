RECOMMENDATION

Build Concept 1 — Digital Architect — and borrow two moves from the others.

Why it wins. Your resume's strongest claim is judgment — knowing where determinism beats a model. The Architect concept's whole visual language (grid, mono labels, case-study rows, system-architecture experience page) says that before anyone reads a word. It scores highest on developer credibility and usability, and its one WebGL scene is cheap: ~70 points and lines, cursor-reactive, pausable.

Why not the others. Editorial is the most beautiful and the cheapest to run, but it reads "design-led" more than "engineering-led" for a backend-heavy senior role. Playground is the most memorable and best shows frontend chops, but every page carries a WebGL cost and a 3D project ring that needs real screenshots to pay off — more risk for a recruiter on a laptop.

Steal these. From Editorial: the full-viewport project spread for the case-study detail view. From Playground: the "enter a project" page transition (Framer Motion layoutId from row → detail). Both fit inside the Architect grid without changing its identity.

Dependencies

Nothing new is required. Optional: lenis (3 KB) for smooth scroll if you want scroll-linked 3D to feel buttery — Framer Motion's useScroll works without it. Avoid GSAP/ScrollTrigger; Framer covers every animation shown here. Fonts: Inter (body, all), JetBrains Mono (labels, all), Space Grotesk (Editorial display), Plus Jakarta Sans (Playground display) — all self-hosted or Google.
