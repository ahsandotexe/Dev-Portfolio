Portfolio direction mockups

Five directions for the same job: in 30 seconds a recruiter knows who Ahsan is, sees proof, and finds the resume or contact. Each is a working home page with the brief's real content, a dark and light theme (toggle in the header), responsive layout and reduced-motion support. Open at 1440 and 375.

01 Status page quiet

The home page is an ops status board: eight real systems, each marked live and public, live and internal, or delivered, with the case study opening in place. Rows check in one after another on load; a timestamp ticks. Atkinson Hyperlegible and IBM Plex Mono; no brand colour, only semantic green and amber on neutral grey.

02 Magazine profile restrained

A feature article about him. Fraunces display with a drop cap, Newsreader text, a double-rule masthead, and marginalia: role, stack, impact and dates sit in the margin beside each item so the main column reads as prose and the proof reads at a glance. Headline words settle into place on load. Cool paper and burgundy; dark mode is aubergine and rose.

03 Blueprint middle

A drafting sheet. Three of his real architectures (NBP account opening, the Lumida platform, Qlu.ai) are drawn as a schematic that draws itself on load; hovering a box lights its connections and names the project. Barlow Semi Condensed, the DIN-like lettering of engineering drawings, with Martian Mono annotations and a title block in the footer. Cobalt on cobalt, no accent colour.

04 Account-opening form real risk

The page is a filled-in bank form, the artifact his products produce. His name types into comb boxes, the fields fill in ballpoint blue, and an approval stamp lands. Numbered sections: applicant, proof of work as attachments, employment history as a table, declared skills as a checkbox grid, correspondence with a signature line. Public Sans labels, Courier Prime values.

05 Swiss poster middle to high

An eight-column poster grid. The headline in expanded Archivo across six cells, the proof figures (300+, 8) as the largest objects on the page, one crimson cell for the resume. Cells wipe in from their own edges on load; tiles invert to black on hover. One typeface at two widths; white, black and crimson; light first.
